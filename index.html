About Our Company

Smart Automation & Connected Living

Smart Home Service Co., Ltd. specializes in designing, engineering, and operating software and hardware ecosystems for residential and commercial automation. We develop mobile applications published on the Google Play Store that allow users to securely control smart switches, lighting, environmental sensors, cameras, and IoT gateways from anywhere.

Our mobile engineering team follows Google Play Developer Quality and Security Guidelines, utilizing state-of-the-art encryption (TLS 1.3), end-to-end device provisioning, minimal permission scopes, and strict user privacy standards adhering to Thailand's PDPA and international data protection laws.
