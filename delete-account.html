Account & Data Deletion Request

Under Google Play policy, users of Smart Home Service applications can request the permanent deletion of their account, device pairings, and associated telemetry.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Personal Identity: Your registered email, name, encrypted passwords, and profile credentials are completely purged from active databases within 30 days.
  • Hardware Disassociation: All paired smart switches, gateways, sensors, and cameras are unlinked from your cloud account.
  • Automation Scenes & Schedules: Any configured timers, automated scenes, and device logs are permanently erased.
  • Data Retention Exceptions: Financial records or transaction invoices (if any) are retained only to the extent mandated by applicable tax and accounting laws in Thailand.

Method 1: In-App Deletion

Open the app → Profile / Settings → Account Security → Select "Delete Account" → Confirm authorization code.

Method 2: Web Submission Form

If you have uninstalled the application, use the form below to request account termination directly.

Submit Deletion Request

Our security team will send a verification link to your registered email to confirm ownership.

Alternatively, email our privacy desk directly at support@yourdomain.com with subject "Account Deletion".
