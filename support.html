Frequently Asked Questions (Troubleshooting)

How do I pair a new smart switch or sensor?

Ensure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled on your Android smartphone. Ensure your phone is connected to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Open the app, tap '+' at the top right, scan the QR code on the hardware or allow Bluetooth discovery.

Why does the app ask for Location permission?

Android OS requires Location permission to scan for Wi-Fi SSIDs and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peripherals. We do not store or track your geographical GPS position.

How do I delete my account and personal data?

You can delete your account directly inside the app settings or via our Account Deletion Web Portal.
