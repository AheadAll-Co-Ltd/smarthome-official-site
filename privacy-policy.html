2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide IoT automation and account services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, encrypted authentication tokens, and profile settings upon account creation.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Mobile device model, operating system version, app version, unique installation identifier, IP address, and crash reports.
  • Smart Home Hardware Information: Connected device identifiers (MAC address, IP address, device firmware version, device status such as power on/off, temperature, schedule configurations).
  • Wireless Network Information (SSID/BSSID): Used strictly during Wi-Fi pairing to configure smart devices onto your home local network.

3. Android Permissions & Technical Justification

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, we disclose the exact reasons for requested runtime permissions:

Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Required by Android OS to scan for nearby Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) smart devices during hardware setup. We do NOT track or store your physical GPS location on our servers.

Nearby Devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)

Enables direct communication with smart home accessories (BLE switches, sensors) for initial pairing.

Camera (CAMERA)

Used exclusively when you scan a QR code on hardware packaging or device labels to register equipment. No video stream or photos are captured or uploaded without explicit action.

Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to alert you in real-time regarding critical home security alarms, sensor alerts, or hardware disconnects.

4. Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We integrate established industry SDKs for core functionality:

  • Google Play Services: Core system APIs, in-app messaging, and license verification.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers push notifications for alerts and automation events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash stack traces to improve app stability.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 for sensitive credentials at rest). Personal data is retained only for as long as your account remains active or as required by statutory recordkeeping obligations.
